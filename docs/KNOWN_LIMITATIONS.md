# Known limitations

- No cloud sync/backup (offline by design); uninstalling wipes data.
- PDF is FR-first with Arabic total line; full RTL contract layout not yet implemented.
- No photos of vehicle damage (planned: CameraX + local storage).
- No electronic signature capture (printed signature lines only).
- Currency fixed to DZD; no multi-currency.
- minSdk 26 (Android 8.0+).
